# Sanitization-Driven, Adversarial-Informed Framework for Network Anomaly Detection

This project was accepted at **eNergetics 2025 (Virtual)**, taking place Nov 24–25, 2025. 

**Co-authors:** Yogesh Sanodiya, Pratik Chouragadey, Soumyajit Ghosh.

## Overview

Network anomaly detection is a critical component of modern cybersecurity, yet many existing machine learning models remain highly vulnerable to adversarial attacks. In this research, we introduce a novel, end-to-end framework designed to build adversarial robustness directly into the anomaly detection pipeline.

Our approach integrates two key strategies:
1. **Adversarial-Informed Training:** We leverage Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to synthesize realistic adversarial network traffic. By training our models on these challenging examples, the system learns to recognize and adapt to sophisticated evasion attempts.
2. **Data Sanitization:** We employ a robust preprocessing pipeline to clean and normalize network traffic data, reducing the noise and ensuring that feature extraction focuses on the most critical behavioral patterns.

## Technical Architecture

The core of our detection pipeline utilizes a deep autoencoder architecture. The autoencoder is trained to compress and reconstruct normal network traffic. When an anomaly or adversarial packet is encountered, the reconstruction error spikes, signaling a potential threat. 

### Key Components

- **Feature Extraction:** Extracting relevant flow-based and packet-level features.
- **GAN-based Generation:** Creating synthetic adversarial traffic to fortify the training dataset.
- **Autoencoder Scoring:** Calculating anomaly scores based on reconstruction loss.
- **Classical ML baselines:** Comparing our approach against traditional algorithms like Random Forest and SVM.

## Impact

By combining adversarial training with rigorous data sanitization, our framework demonstrates a significant improvement in detecting sophisticated network attacks without sacrificing accuracy on normal traffic. This research pushes the boundaries of adversarial robustness in network security systems.
